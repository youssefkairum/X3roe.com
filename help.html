01 About the App

Joke Generator is a macOS app by Youssef Keram, who publishes as X3roe. The current version is 2.0, and it requires macOS 26 or later.

02 Updates

Joke Generator updates itself through its built-in updater, so you don’t need to download new versions from this website.

03 License Activation

  • To activate Joke Generator, you need your license key and an internet connection.
  • If activation doesn’t go through, check that your Mac is connected to the internet and try again.
  • If the app shows “Activation limit reached”, contact support and we’ll help you sort it out.

04 Privacy

To learn what data Joke Generator sends when it checks for updates and when it activates, checks or deactivates your license, see the Privacy Policy. Use of the app is covered by the EULA.
