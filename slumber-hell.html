Tactical Horror

Core Gameplay & Mechanics.

You cannot fight back. Your only hope is your hearing, your wits, and whatever hiding spots you can find before the footsteps reach your door.

Binaural Spatial Audio

Listen closely. Binaural spatial audio allows you to pinpoint the Entity's movement through creaking floorboards and echoing hallways.

Tactical Hiding System

Hide inside wooden wardrobes, slip under metal beds, and crouch behind furniture. The Entity dynamically inspects rooms if it suspects you nearby.

Chilling Escape Puzzles

Search shadowy rooms for hidden keys, tools, and unlock sealed doors to secure your escape route as tension escalates.

Unpredictable Stalker AI

No two playthroughs are identical. The Entity patrols dynamically, investigates disturbances, and adapts its search pattern.
