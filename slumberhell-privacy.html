01 Introduction & Scope

Slumber Hell (“the Game”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is handled when you play the Game on any of its supported platforms:

  • iOS, downloaded from the Apple App Store;
  • Android, downloaded from Google Play;
  • Windows PC, downloaded as a ZIP file from dl.x3roe.com (linked from the Slumber Hell page).

Google AdMob advertising (section 03), Apple Game Center and Google Play Games Services (section 04), and in-app purchases through the App Store or Google Play (section 06) apply only to the mobile versions (iOS and Android). Google AdMob is not part of the Windows PC version. The x3roe.com website itself is covered by the site Privacy Policy.

02 Data Collection & Storage

  • Gameplay progress, audio settings, inventory, and local best survival times are stored locally on your device.
  • The mobile versions integrate third-party services (Google AdMob, Google Play Games Services, and Apple Game Center) which may collect data as described below.

03 Advertising & Tracking (iOS & Android)

  • In the iOS and Android versions, we use Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. AdMob is not used in the Windows PC version.
  • AdMob may collect and process certain data (like device identifiers, Android Advertising ID, and IP addresses) to provide personalized advertising. View Google's Privacy Policy (opens in new tab).
  • On iOS, we will request your permission via the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before tracking you for personalized ads. On Android, you may opt-out of personalized ads via your device settings.

04 Game Center & Play Games Services (iOS & Android)

  • The mobile versions integrate with Apple Game Center (iOS) and Google Play Games Services (Android) to provide achievements and global leaderboards. These services are not part of the Windows PC version.
  • If you sign in to these services, your gameplay progress, scores, and achievements are synced with Apple or Google's servers subject to their respective privacy policies.

05 Audio & Spatial Rendering

  • Slumber Hell utilizes Binaural 3D Spatial Audio to render real-time soundscapes and Entity movement.
  • The Game does not record, capture, or transmit microphone input or ambient room audio.

06 Purchases & Distribution

  • In the mobile versions, any game purchases (like removing ads) are processed directly through platform store systems (Apple App Store or Google Play Billing).
  • The Windows PC version is distributed as a direct ZIP download from dl.x3roe.com.
  • We do not collect, process, or store credit card numbers, billing addresses, or payment tokens.

07 Analytics & Stability

Anonymous crash logs and basic device telemetry provided by platform storefront tools may be utilized solely for debugging crashes and optimizing rendering performance.

08 Data Deletion

  • Local Data: All local game data (progress, settings) can be permanently deleted by uninstalling the Game from your device.
  • Cloud Data: To request the deletion of data synced to Google Play Games Services or Apple Game Center, you may manage or delete your gaming profiles directly within your Google Account or Apple ID settings.
  • If you require further assistance regarding data deletion, please contact us at the email provided below.

09 Children’s Privacy

Slumber Hell contains survival horror themes and dark atmospheric tension. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under 13.

10 Policy Updates

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy as new game features or platform updates are introduced. Changes will be posted on this page, and the “Last updated” date at the top will change.
