01 Introduction

Slumber Hell (“the Game”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is handled when you play the Game across supported platforms.

02 Data Collection & Storage

  • Gameplay progress, audio settings, inventory, and local best survival times are stored locally on your device.
  • The Game integrates third-party services (such as Google AdMob and Apple Game Center) which may collect data as described below.

03 Advertising & Tracking

  • We use Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads.
  • AdMob may collect and process certain data (like device identifiers and IP addresses) to provide personalized advertising. On iOS, we will request your permission via the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before tracking you for personalized ads.

04 Game Center & Leaderboards

  • The Game integrates with Apple Game Center to provide achievements and global leaderboards (e.g., escape times).
  • If you sign in to Game Center, your gameplay progress, scores, and achievements are synced with Apple's servers subject to Apple's privacy policies.

05 Audio & Spatial Rendering

  • Slumber Hell utilizes Binaural 3D Spatial Audio to render real-time soundscapes and Entity movement.
  • The Game does not record, capture, or transmit microphone input or ambient room audio.

06 Purchases & Distribution

  • Any game purchases (like removing ads) are processed directly through platform store systems (e.g., Apple App Store).
  • We do not collect, process, or store credit card numbers, billing addresses, or payment tokens.

07 Analytics & Stability

Anonymous crash logs and basic device telemetry provided by platform storefront tools may be utilized solely for debugging crashes and optimizing rendering performance.

08 Children’s Privacy

Slumber Hell contains survival horror themes and dark atmospheric tension. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under 13.

09 Policy Updates

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy as new game features or platform updates are introduced. Changes will be posted on this page.
