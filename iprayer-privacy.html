01 Introduction

iPrayer (“the App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled when you use the App.

02 Data Collection

  • The App does not collect or share your personal data with us or third parties.
  • Your settings, bookmarks, reading position, tasbih counts and prayer tracker are stored on your device.
  • If you turn on the App’s optional iCloud sync, this data is also stored in your own iCloud account so it stays in sync across your devices.
  • iPrayer runs no servers, and this data is never sent to us.

03 Location Services

  • To provide accurate prayer times and Qibla direction, the App requires access to your device's Location Services.
  • Location data is processed locally on your device to perform mathematical calculations.
  • We never log, store, or transmit your location data to any external server.

04 Notifications

  • The App uses the iOS User Notifications framework to schedule prayer alerts.
  • These alerts are generated and scheduled entirely locally on your device.

05 Children’s Privacy

The App does not collect personal data from any user (see section 02). We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

06 Changes to Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be reflected in the App or on our website.
