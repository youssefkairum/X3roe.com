01 Introduction

At X3ROE (the development alias of Youssef Keram), we engineer minimalist, native experiences with a strict adherence to user privacy. This policy covers all apps and services under the X3ROE ecosystem, including iOS apps (iPasscoder, iPrayer), games (Voxel Tennis, Slumber Hell), and web infrastructure (X3roePlay, X3roeDrive).

02 App-Specific Privacy

  • iPasscoder: Operates on military-grade encryption. Your passwords and secure data are encrypted entirely on-device. We have zero access to your vault or master passwords.
  • iPrayer: Location data is used strictly locally on your device to calculate accurate prayer times. We do not transmit or store your location on our servers.
  • Games: We do not track personal gameplay data in our games (such as Voxel Tennis, Slumber Hell, or GhostOps: Mobile Warfare). Any leaderboards or multiplayer functions utilize standard Apple Game Center APIs or our minimal X3roePlay infrastructure without tracking identifiable telemetry.

03 Web & Infrastructure

For services like X3roeDrive, X3roePlay, and our multiplayer servers, we log only essential technical data (such as IP addresses and connection timestamps) required to maintain server stability, prevent attacks, and route traffic securely. We never sell or monetize your data.

04 Security

We implement robust security measures across all platforms. Our apps leverage Apple's native Secure Enclave where applicable, and our web APIs are secured behind enterprise-grade firewalls.
