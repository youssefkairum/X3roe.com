01 Introduction

X3roe is the name under which Youssef Keram publishes apps, games and music. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Youssef Keram. This policy explains how data is handled on the x3roe.com website and across X3roe apps and services, including iOS apps (iPasscoder, iPrayer), games (Voxel Tennis, Slumber Hell, GhostOps: Mobile Warfare), the macOS app Joke Generator, and web infrastructure (X3roePlay, X3roeDrive).

03 Apps & Games

  • iPasscoder: Your vault (your saved passcodes and other secure data) is encrypted and stored on your device. We have no access to your vault or master password. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob; Premium is ad-free (see Advertising).
  • iPrayer: Location data is used only on your device to calculate accurate prayer times. We do not transmit or store your location on our servers.
  • Games: Leaderboards, achievements and multiplayer features use Apple Game Center, Google Play Games Services (Slumber Hell on Android) or our X3roePlay infrastructure. Apart from the advertising described below, we do not track personal gameplay data in Voxel Tennis or Slumber Hell. Slumber Hell is covered in detail by its own policy (see Per-App Policies). For our other games, each game’s App Store privacy label lists the following:
    • Voxel Tennis: diagnostic data that is not linked to your identity: performance data, used for analytics, and crash data and other diagnostic data, used for app functionality.
    • GhostOps: Mobile Warfare: gameplay content that is linked to your identity and used for app functionality. It also lists data that is not linked to your identity: diagnostics (crash, performance and other diagnostic data), used for analytics, and your name and product-interaction data, used for app functionality.
  • Joke Generator (macOS): To check for updates, the app requests https://x3roe.com/appcast.xml, which is served like the rest of this website (see This Website). When you activate or deactivate a license, and when the app checks that your license is valid, it sends your license key and details of that activation (the activation instance) to Lemon Squeezy (api.lemonsqueezy.com). Lemon Squeezy’s response can include the name and email address linked to your purchase. Lemon Squeezy handles that data under its own privacy policy. For help with the app, see Joke Generator Help.

04 Advertising

Some of our apps are free and supported by ads. These apps use Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google:

  • iPasscoder: the free version on iOS. iPasscoder Premium is ad-free and does not load AdMob.
  • Slumber Hell: the iOS and Android versions. The Windows PC version does not include AdMob.

In these apps, AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as Apple’s Identifier for Advertisers, or IDFA, and the Android Advertising ID), your IP address and similar technical data to serve and personalize ads.

On iOS, these apps ask for your permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt before your IDFA can be used for tracking. You can decline, or change your choice later in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. On Android, you can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

Google uses this data as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy. The per-app policies above describe the ads in each app in more detail.

05 This Website

x3roe.com is a static website. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. To deliver the site and protect it from abuse, GitHub and Cloudflare process your IP address and standard request information (such as the page or file requested, your browser’s user agent and the time of the request) in their request logs. Downloads from dl.x3roe.com are also served through Cloudflare.

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), so Google receives your IP address and user agent when your browser fetches them.

The site does not use analytics and does not set any cookies itself. If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message, and use them to reply. General questions and questions about this website go to contact@x3roe.com. App support for iPasscoder, iPrayer, Slumber Hell and Joke Generator is at support@x3roe.com.

Messages sent through the contact and support forms on x3roe.com are processed by Formspree (formspree.io). When you send a form, Formspree receives the name, email address and message you enter, plus technical data such as your IP address, forwards your message to us by email, and keeps a copy of it in our Formspree account. We use it to reply to you. If JavaScript is turned off in your browser, Formspree may show its own spam check (such as Google reCAPTCHA) before accepting the message. Formspree handles this data under its own privacy policy.

Our mailbox is hosted by Zoho Mail. Emails you send to contact@x3roe.com or support@x3roe.com, and messages sent through the forms, are stored there.

More information: GitHub Privacy Statement, Cloudflare Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy, Formspree Privacy Policy, Zoho Privacy Policy.

06 Web Services

For services like X3roeDrive, X3roePlay, and our multiplayer servers, we log only essential technical data (such as IP addresses and connection timestamps) required to maintain server stability, prevent attacks, and route traffic securely. We do not sell or monetize these logs.

07 Security

We implement robust security measures across all platforms. Our apps leverage Apple’s native Secure Enclave where applicable, and our web APIs are secured behind enterprise-grade firewalls.
